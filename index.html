Privacy Policy — Surah Al-Mulk Hifz

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Surah Al-Mulk Hifz is a free, offline Quran memorisation application designed to help users memorise Surah Al-Mulk and study its translations.

Sadaqah Jariyah

This application is offered completely free of charge as an act of Sadaqah Jariyah, with the intention of seeking reward from Allah.

This app has been created in loving memory of the developer's deceased mother and brother.

We humbly request every user who benefits from this application to remember them in their prayers and make dua that Allah forgives their sins, has mercy upon them, grants them peace, and admits them into Jannatul Firdaus.

May Allah accept this effort as Sadaqah Jariyah, allow its benefit to reach those who use it, and grant its reward to those for whom this work has been dedicated. Ameen.

Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. This application is designed to operate entirely offline and does not require users to create an account or sign up.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or other personal information is collected.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No device identifiers are collected for tracking.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers are used.
  • No analytics or tracking services are used.
  • No personal data is transmitted over the internet.

Data Storage

Memorisation progress and application settings are stored locally on the user's device using the application's local storage mechanisms.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

Permissions

The application does not request any Android permissions.

Internet Access

The application is designed to function offline and does not require an internet connection to use its core features.

Third-Party Services

The application does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or personal-data collection services.

Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
